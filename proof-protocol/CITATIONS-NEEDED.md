## What closing C1-gotsman took, and the one thing it corrected

The prediction was that Huang's **first two pages** would do it, because his introduction has to state
the Gotsman–Linial reduction to explain why the theorem settles the conjecture. It did, as **Theorem
1.3**, and the paste closed four rows rather than two: `C1-gotsman`; `C1-bound` upgraded to primary
tier, with CFGS's base-2 logarithm *and* their matching ⌈√n⌉ construction; plus new rows
`C1-tightness` and `C1-chain`. Ledger **P-26**.

**It also corrected my statement of what was needed.** I asked for the equivalence as *"deg(f) ≤
poly(s(f)) iff every induced subgraph on more than 2^(n−1) vertices has maximum degree n^Ω(1)"*.
Gotsman–Linial state it with **Γ(H) = max{Δ(H), Δ(Q_n − H)}**, under **|V(H)| ≠ 2^(n−1)**, for a
general monotone *h* — the "more than half" step is Huang's *bridge* to h = √n, not part of the
equivalence. The row is closed on the source's form, not on mine.
