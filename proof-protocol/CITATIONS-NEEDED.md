# The seven citations I cannot check from here

Every egress route is blocked from this container — `arxiv.org`, `export.arxiv.org`,
`api.crossref.org`, `doi.org`, `jstor.org`, `link.springer.com`, `ams.org`. Web search works and was
used for everything it could settle (23 rows verified). These seven are what is left, and each needs
only a sentence or two from a source you can reach.

**Please do not paraphrase from memory** — that is the layer that has already produced two real
errors in this project (C2 was shorthand that became an error in context; C5 was a missing regime).
Pasting the abstract, or the one sentence that states the bound, is enough.

| row | what I need | why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| **C3-shannon** | Shannon 1956: confirmation that the pre-Lovász state was √5 ≤ Θ(C5) ≤ 5/2, and that the upper bound was the fractional clique cover α\*. | It is the before-board for case 3, which is the diagnostic's one principled false negative. |
| **C4 (half)** | Weaver's KS₂ / Anderson paving: confirmation that the matrix-Chernoff route needs δ ≲ 1/log n. The MSS after-board is already verified. | It is the before-board bound for case 4, a positive the diagnostic fires on. |
| **C7** | Goldston–Pintz–Yıldırım: that the method needs level of distribution θ > 1/2, and that Bombieri–Vinogradov gives exactly 1/2. Plus Maynard arXiv:1311.4600 and Zhang 2013 for the two after-boards. | Case 7 is the one case with two accepted answers; the rubric depends on both being real. |
| **C9** | The classical pre-Wooley Vinogradov mean-value bound s ≳ c·k² log k. **And** independent confirmation that Wooley's efficient congruencing is arXiv:1101.0574, posted 2011-01-03. | The posting date is what voided case 9 in L2, and I took it from the task list rather than a source. |
| **C10** | Fortnow and/or Feige's counterexamples to multiplicativity of game value, and Raz 1995 / Holenstein 2007 for the after-board. | Case 10 is a positive the diagnostic fires on, and its target_law = "none" assignment rests on these counterexamples. |
| **C12** | Perelman math/0211159: that W-entropy and reduced volume give κ-noncollapsing, and that this is what controls collapsing at singular times. | Case 12 is the "same move, new invariant" case that fits no transformation. |
| **N3** | Behrend 1946: that the construction excludes power savings for 3AP-free subsets of [N]. | N3 is the **discriminator** — case 2's twin, and the item that shows the rebuilt diagnostic can separate a positive from its matched twin. |

**N3 is the one I would most like.** It is load-bearing for the only within-family discrimination the
rebuilt diagnostic makes. Note that the diagnostic's *use* of N3 rests on the absence of a product
structure on `[N]`, which is structural and does not depend on Behrend — but the twin's status as a
negative does.

Two further rows are declared STANDARD rather than fetched and are lower priority:
**N16** (that border rank is submultiplicative under tensor product and ω is defined via a limit of
tensor powers) and **N20** (Kollár–Rónyai–Szabó / Alon–Rónyai–Szabó norm graphs matching
Kővári–Sós–Turán for small t).
